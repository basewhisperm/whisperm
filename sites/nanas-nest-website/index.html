Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

A few of the questions we hear most from families across DC, Maryland & Northern Virginia.

View the Full FAQ
How is a night nurse different from a postpartum doula?
A night nurse (or newborn care specialist) focuses on hands-on overnight infant care so you can rest; a postpartum doula focuses more broadly on your recovery, feeding support, and light household help. Read the full comparison.
Why is Nana's Nest referral-based?
Referrals let us keep our roster small, our matching process personal, and our families' privacy protected. If you weren't referred, you're still welcome to reach out — start here.
When should we start sleep training?
Most families begin structured sleep training between 4 and 6 months, though gentle sleep shaping can start from birth. See our approach by age.

Ready to request an introduction?

Tell us about your family and due date, and we'll follow up personally within 1–2 business days.
