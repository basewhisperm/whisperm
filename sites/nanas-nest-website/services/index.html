Services

Concierge Newborn Care & Sleep Support

Two complementary services, one continuous standard of care — from your first night home to your baby's first full night of independent sleep.

Newborn Night Nursing

Overnight, in-home newborn care from an RN-caliber caregiver — typically ages birth to 12–16 weeks.

  • Overnight feedings, soothing & diapering
  • Feeding coordination (breast, bottle, or combination)
  • Postpartum recovery support for parents
  • Twins, multiples & NICU-graduate experience
Learn About Night Nursing

Sleep Training & Coaching

A personalized, gentle sleep plan — typically ages 4 months through the toddler years.

  • In-depth sleep assessment
  • Custom, written sleep plan
  • Nightly text & phone support
  • In-home or virtual consultations
