Sleep Training & Coaching

Gentle, Evidence-Based Sleep Training for Babies & Toddlers

A personalized sleep plan built around your baby's temperament, your parenting philosophy, and your household — never a rigid, one-size-fits-all method.

Sleep training with Nana's Nest starts with listening — to your baby's sleep history, your goals, and what you're and aren't comfortable trying. From there, our certified pediatric sleep consultants build a written, step-by-step plan and stay with you through every night of it.

Our Approach

We favor gentle, responsive methods over rigid extinction-based ones, but every plan is genuinely customized — not a template with your baby's name filled in. If you're weighing your options, our guide to gentle sleep training methods, explained is a good place to start.

Ages & Stages We Support

  • Newborn Sleep Shaping (0–4 months) — healthy habits and realistic expectations, no formal "training" yet
  • Sleep Training (4–18 months) — teaching independent, self-settled sleep
  • Toddler Transitions (18 months–4 years) — crib-to-bed moves, nap changes, regressions
  • Twins & Multiples — coordinated plans for shared rooms and staggered schedules

What's Included

  • An in-depth intake & sleep assessment
  • A personalized, written sleep plan
  • Nightly text and phone support during the first 1–2 weeks
  • In-home or virtual consultations, whichever fits your family
  • Follow-up check-ins as new challenges come up

Common Challenges We Help With

The 4-month sleep regression, frequent night waking, early-morning waking, nap refusal, the crib-to-bed transition, night weaning, and getting back on track after travel or a schedule disruption.

Serving the DMV, In-Home or Virtually

We work with families throughout Washington, DC, Montgomery County, MD, and Northern Virginia — and with families anywhere via virtual consultation.

Newborn Under 12 Weeks?

Many families start with overnight night nursing support first, then transition into a sleep training plan. See our newborn night nursing services.
