Version: 1.0

Status: Constitutional Document

# Purpose

This document defines the long-term vision and architectural philosophy of WhispeRM.

It is not an implementation guide.

It is not a technical specification.

It is the constitutional document that every future architectural decision should align with.

Whenever implementation details conflict with this document, the implementation should be questioned—not the vision.

---

# Mission

WhispeRM exists to automate the acquisition of marketplace sellers.

Businesses should no longer spend their mornings searching marketplaces, identifying sellers, sending invitations, following up, and manually entering data into CRM systems.

WhispeRM performs this work continuously.

The CRM is not the destination.

CRM is the handoff point where successful acquisition becomes customer relationships and revenue.

---

# Product Definition

WhispeRM is an Autonomous Acquisition Platform.

It continuously discovers, qualifies, prioritizes, acquires, and converts marketplace sellers into customer relationships.

The platform replaces repetitive acquisition work with autonomous software.

# Manual Mode vs Autonomous Mode

WhispeRM operates in two modes.

## Manual Mode

Manual Mode is the trial experience.

The customer controls every action.

The platform assists but never acts autonomously.

Example workflow:

Campaign

↓

Paste Marketplace URL

↓

Run Discovery

↓

Review Candidates

↓

Send Invitation

↓

Claim

↓

CRM

Manual execution demonstrates value.

---

## Autonomous Mode

Autonomous Mode is the premium operating mode.

The customer defines strategy once.

The platform performs acquisition continuously.

Campaign Strategy

↓

Discovery Jobs

↓

Discovery Runs

↓

Qualification

↓

Ranking

↓

Invitation

↓

Claim

↓

CRM

The customer wakes up to new opportunities every morning.

Automation is the premium product.

Manual execution is the evaluation experience.

# Campaign

Campaign is not a folder.

Campaign is not a dashboard.

Campaign is not a reporting object.

Campaign is an autonomous business strategy.

A campaign defines:

- target marketplaces
- categories
- locations
- seller profile
- acquisition strategy
- qualification policy
- invitation policy
- automation policy

Campaigns own acquisition.

Everything else executes the campaign.

---

# Autonomous Workers

Every campaign behaves like an autonomous acquisition employee.

Instead of assigning work to salespeople, businesses assign work to campaigns.

Campaigns continuously perform:

- discovery
- qualification
- prioritization
- invitation
- follow-up
- conversion

without requiring manual intervention.
