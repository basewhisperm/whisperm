# Purpose

The Business Growth Opportunity Engine is the core capability of WhispeRM.

Its responsibility is to continuously discover, evaluate, qualify, prioritize, supply, and improve Business Growth Opportunities for every active Campaign.

Every major subsystem of WhispeRM either contributes to this engine or realizes the value it creates.

---

# Vision

Businesses should never begin the day searching for opportunity.

Instead, every active Campaign continuously supplies Business Growth Opportunities while customers focus on judgment, relationships, negotiation, and growth.

WhispeRM works before its customers do.

# Campaign Responsibility

Campaigns own business intent.

Campaigns never perform work.

Campaigns define work.

Campaigns define:

- business objectives
- target marketplaces
- categories
- geography
- seller profile
- qualification policy
- acquisition policy
- automation policy
- scheduling
- success metrics

Workers execute Campaign intent.

---

# Marketplace Intelligence

Marketplace Intelligence continuously transforms marketplace signals into Potential Opportunities.

Responsibilities include:

- marketplace discovery
- listing extraction
- seller identification
- deduplication
- enrichment
- normalization
- trend detection

Output:

Potential Opportunities

# Seller Intelligence

Seller Intelligence continuously evaluates Potential Opportunities.

Responsibilities include:

- seller qualification
- confidence scoring
- opportunity scoring
- policy evaluation
- campaign matching
- prioritization

Output:

Business Growth Opportunities

# Autonomous Acquisition

- messaging
- follow-up
- retries
- escalation
- claim
- conversion

Automation policy determines the level of autonomy.

# Engineering Rule

Every capability merged into WhispeRM should satisfy at least one of the following:

- Increase Business Growth Opportunities
- Improve Business Growth Opportunity quality
- Reduce Business Growth Opportunity acquisition cost
- Increase Business Growth Opportunity velocity
- Improve Business Growth Opportunity conversion
- Increase platform learning

Capabilities that satisfy none of these should receive lower architectural priority.
